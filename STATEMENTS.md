# Problem Statement

Small clinics and hospital front desks often manage patient records manually, using paper registers or scattered spreadsheets. This makes it slow to look up a patient's history, track which doctor they have been assigned to, or check whether their consultation is complete.

## Scope
This project implements a lightweight, console-based patient record system for front-desk use. It focuses on core record-keeping (registration, search, doctor assignment, consultation tracking) rather than full hospital management. Billing, appointments, and login systems are out of scope.

## Target Users
- Hospital or clinic front-desk staff
- Small clinics without dedicated hospital management software

## High-Level Features
- Patient registration with validated input
- Patient lookup by ID
- Doctor and department assignment
- Consultation status tracking
- Full patient list view
